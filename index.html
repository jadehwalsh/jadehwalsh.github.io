Jade Hainsworth-Walsh

Jade is a Northern writer and artist based in London.

She has a PhD in English and Creative Writing from Royal Holloway, University of London, where she has lectured in literature and writing. Her interdisciplinary research explores the crossroads of art, literature, image response theory, and the gothic through ekphrasis.

She is the author of several gothic poems and is currently working on her debut novel.
